Drafting in League of Legends is a strategic phase that sets the tone for every match. Selecting champions while considering team composition, lane matchups, and enemy picks helps shape the win condition before the game even begins. A good draft balances damage types, crowd control, engage tools, wave clear, and frontline durability.

Ideal squads often include a strong frontline with reliable engage, a backline carry that can be protected, and support champions that enable both early skirmishes and late-game scaling. Counterpicking is also vital; it can neutralize enemy threats and force them into uncomfortable roles.

Communication during draft is key. Sharing priorities, preferred champions, and flexible pick order allows teams to adapt around bans and opponent selections. When drafting, think about power spikes, objective control, and the team’s ability to rotate effectively.

A cohesive composition can turn even average mechanical play into a solid performance. Focus on synergy, diversity, and game plan clarity, and your team will be far better positioned for success from champion select onward.
